About Me

I'm Ola Ayman Saad, a passionate Management Information Systems (MIS) student with a strong interest in software development, web design, and digital marketing. I began my programming journey by developing creative C# applications like calculators, alarm clocks, wall clocks, and geometry area calculators. I’ve also studied digital marketing and created campaigns and promotional pages for courses and products, including a C# course page and a product page for “Mood Coffee at the Right Time.” Additionally, I design and build web pages using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript—some of my work includes an online library, a music learning website, a language learning site, and an online shop for modest fashion. I enjoy blending technology and creativity to create meaningful user experiences, and my goal is to work in a dynamic tech environment or launch my own startup one day.
